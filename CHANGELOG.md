## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-08

The first public release.

### Added

- Plays *Countdown to Doomsday* (DOS version 1.00) natively on Linux and
  Windows, from your own copy of the game.
- VGA graphics, AdLib music and the sound effects as the original plays
  them on a Tandy 1000.
- Full screen, 4:3 aspect correction, a combat speed and an options screen
  (OPTIONS.md).
- The Log Book's entries and maps shown in the game, opened only with your
  own game files.
- Gamepad support, including typing at the name prompts.
- Saves kept beside the program; saves from the DOS game carried over.
- Three of the original's bugs that lose items or characters, fixed.
