# Options

Your options, where to change them, and what each is set to until you change it.
The options screen opens with Shift+F12 anywhere in the game. README.md lists
everything that differs from the original game.

## Text

- **Message speed** — Options screen (the original's CHANGE, Messages). Default: as your saved game has it. How long the game's messages stay on screen.
- **Text style** — Options screen. Default: Instant. Shows each message at once, not letter by letter as the original does.

## Fights

- **Combat speed** — Options screen, or F12 to cycle. Default: Default (original pace). Makes fights 1.25, 1.5, 2 or 4 times faster.

## Window

- **Aspect** — Options screen, or Shift+F11. Default: Exact. Exact keeps square pixels; 4:3 stretches the picture as a 1990 monitor showed it.
- **Full screen** — Options screen, or F11 or Alt+Enter. Default: Off. Fills the screen with the game, keeping its shape.
- **Animation** — Options screen (the original's CHANGE, Anim). Default: as your saved game has it. Whether the game's pictures animate.
- **Options screen** — Shift+F12, or Options in the team menu (in place of JOYSTICK/MOUSE INITIALIZE). Opens this list from anywhere in the game, takes no game time and remembers your choices.

## Sound

- **Effects** — Options screen. Default: Tandy. Tandy plays the effects as the original does on a Tandy 1000; AdLib plays their AdLib versions; Off silences them.
- **Volume** — Options screen. Default: 6. Sets how loud the music and effects are, from 0 (muted) to 10.

## Gamepad

- **Button keys** — Options screen, one row per button. Default: the keys in README.md's gamepad table; the triggers give none. Gives any button or trigger another key; a gamepad works whenever one is connected.
